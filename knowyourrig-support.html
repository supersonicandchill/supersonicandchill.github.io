Your data

Know Your Rig stores everything locally on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, and nothing is collected or shared. Make a backup from the app before deleting it if you want to keep your rigs and photos.

Common questions

How many rigs can I build for free?
One. Full Access is a one-time purchase that unlocks additional rigs and importing rigs shared by your crew.

I already bought Full Access on another device or reinstalled.
Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I share a rig with my crew?
Use Share in the app to send the rig file. Crew members import it from Files; importing needs Full Access.

I lost my rigs when I reinstalled.
Data is local-only, so uninstalling erases it. Back up first from the app.
