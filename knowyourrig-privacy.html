Know Your Rig — Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

The short version: Know Your Rig does not collect, store on a server, transmit, or share any personal data, from you or about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ad networks, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

What the app stores

Your rigs, compartment photos, tool labels, quiz progress, and settings are saved only on your own device. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to any server, and is not accessible to the developer or anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is erased unless you saved a backup file yourself.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera and photo library access only so you can photograph or choose pictures of your rig compartments. Those pictures stay on your device inside the app.

Sharing and backups you create

The app lets you back up your rigs and share a rig with your crew as a file using Apple's share sheet. Where that file goes afterward (Messages, Files, email, etc.) is controlled entirely by you and the app you choose to send it with. Importing a shared rig reads the file on your device only.

Purchases

Know Your Rig is free, with one rig included. An optional one-time in-app purchase (Full Access) unlocks additional rigs and importing shared rigs. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details or any personal information from the purchase.

Children's privacy

Know Your Rig does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
