The Irreducible Complexity for Success

The 3 Words That Help You Find It

Danny Cancino

An irreducible complexity is a system made up of multiple, necessary, and interacting parts where the removal of any single one of the parts causes the entire system to quit working. What is it about success that makes it seem so elusive? Is it a magic formula? Is someone gatekeeping the secret? No one is self-made. That's a myth wrapped in narcissism. But there are some traits you can take on that will increase your odds of finding success. This fast read, under an hour for most people, guides you through the three characteristics that are so common in successful people. It reads like a conversation rather than a lecture, and it's the result of over 20 years of observing what increases the odds of success in the workplace and in life.
