Fire Drill Wheel

Random drills for shift crews.

Stop running the same three drills every shift. Fire Drill Wheel picks company training for you: spin the wheel and get a drill. It draws from a built-in library of 115 drills (77 main drills and 38 Sets & Reps drills) across three switchable modes. Built by a career firefighter.

  • 115 drills: 77 main + 38 Sets & Reps
  • Three switchable modes
  • No data collected

Price: Free. $4.99 one-time unlock to customize the wheel. · iOS 13.0 or later · Version 1.7

Health & Fitness · iPhone

Not Today...

Break the habit. One button a day.

A one-button habit-recovery app. Pick what you're walking away from, then tap once each day. The button is red until you confirm and green once you do. Miss a day and the streak resets, but your best streak and total days never do. There is no shame, just get back up. An optional faith mode adds a gentle "Do you want to be healed?" prompt (John 5:6). No accounts, no ads, and your data stays on your device.

  • One button, one day at a time
  • Optional faith mode
  • No accounts, no ads

Price: Free · iOS 15.0 or later · Version 1.1

Graveyard, Called It!

See it. Call it. Score it.

The backseat road-trip game your family already plays, now with a scoreboard. First one to spot a graveyard shouts it and taps their name to score. The app keeps a running tally for every player, trip after trip. Add as many players as you want, fix a mistake with a tap, and enjoy a little ghost-float celebration with every point. No ads, no accounts, no internet required.

  • Running tally per player
  • Works with no internet
  • No ads, no accounts

Price: Free · iOS 15.0 or later
