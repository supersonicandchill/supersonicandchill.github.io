About

The potato behind it.

Danny Cancino, career firefighter and paramedic, builder of apps, writer, musician.

I'm Danny Cancino. By day I'm a firefighter and paramedic. The rest of the time I build apps, write books, make music, narrate audiobooks and edit video.

The logo has a story. I asked an AI to roast the filter I had on my face. It went on about how I looked like a grumpy potato. I said there was no filter, so that was the honest opinion, and we built a logo from it. The taco? I'm often found hanging out with tacos.
